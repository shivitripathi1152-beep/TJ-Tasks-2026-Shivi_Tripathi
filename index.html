WHY STUDYSYNC

Everything you need to stay on track.

College students routinely juggle assignments, exam schedules, project deadlines, and personal milestones across 4 or 5 different apps. StudySync consolidates your academic life into one seamless, distraction-free environment.

Stay Organized

Keep courses, syllabus notes, assignments, and reading materials neatly structured by subject in a clean central dashboard.

Never Miss a Deadline

Intelligent notifications and priority deadline trackers ensure submission dates never catch you unprepared again.

Track Your Progress

Visualize your daily study habits, track GPA targets, monitor course completion rates, and celebrate academic milestones.

Study Smarter

Leverage built-in Pomodoro timers, spaced repetition schedules, and automated study planners tailored to your learning style.

POWERFUL FEATURES

Built specifically for student success.

Every feature in StudySync is designed to minimize cognitive load, eliminate last-minute cramming, and keep your focus on learning.

Smart Task Management

"Keep assignments and academic tasks organized."

Categorize tasks by course, difficulty, and priority. Group sub-tasks, tag homework vs exams, and filter items with a single click.

Deadline Planner

"See upcoming deadlines before they become stressful."

A dynamic calendar and timeline view maps out upcoming exams, paper due dates, and major project milestones weeks in advance.

Study Goals

"Set goals and track your progress."

Break down large academic ambitions (like achieving a target GPA or completing 50 practice tests) into actionable weekly targets.

AI Study Assistant

"Get personalized help with planning and studying."

Receive intelligent study schedule recommendations based on your workload, estimated task difficulty, and upcoming exams.
